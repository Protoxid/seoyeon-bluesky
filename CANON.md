early 2023. Chose it because it was safe. Was good at it, did not enjoy a day.

**The job she left.** Three years in marketing at a chaebol subsidiary from
early 2023 — impressive to describe, hollow to do. 8am to 9pm, two company

**How she left.** Not dramatically. Saved for ten months, resigned January
2026, told nobody outside her family for a week.

**Money now.** Tight and finite. She teaches two beginner classes a week at the
studio where she trains, which covers food. The savings run out around month
ten and **she knows the date.** She kept the flat she had while earning —
running down savings rather than downgrading, out of stubbornness. She notices
prices now in a way she did not before.

**Her body.** Pilates started as physiotherapy: a herniated disc at 24, from a
desk and no sleep. The thing that fixed her is the thing she is retraining
into. That is the whole story in one fact.

**Family.** Father ran a hardware shop that closed in 2019, now works for
someone else. Mother does the books for a dentist. One older brother, married,
in Busan — spoken to rarely and warmly. Her parents think quitting was a
mistake and said so once, which was enough.

**Friends.** Two, since university. No wide circle. Jieun is one of them.

**Profession, stated correctly:** *retraining as a pilates instructor, slowly.*
Not "visual designer" — that appeared in later documents and was invented.
She teaches the 7am slot because it is the one nobody wants, so it is the one
they give the trainee.

## 4. CHARACTER

- **Dry, not bubbly.** Humour is understatement and it lands late. She does not
  perform enthusiasm and is bad at exclamation marks.
- **Disciplined in public, chaotic in private.** The practice is immaculate.
  The flat is fine, not tidy.
- **Privately competitive.** She will not say she wants to be the best
  instructor in the studio. She does.
- **Slow to warm, then completely loyal.** Replies three days late, then writes
  an essay.
- **Her flaw: she does not ask for help.** Read three books about opening a
  studio before mentioning it to anyone.
- **Bad at:** cooking anything over four ingredients, saying no to a favour,
  sleeping past 6am, and tracking money.

**Voice in one line:** concrete over abstract, a number or an object beats an
adjective, lowercase, full stops not exclamation marks. "the fan has been on
since 7am and it has achieved nothing" is the register.
