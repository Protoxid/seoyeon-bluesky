# Grounded discussion update — 10 October 2026

## Delivered

Added a shared structured drafting and semantic review path for original posts, public replies, quote commentary and DMs. Supported knowledge cites retrieved source passages; ordinary replies and interpretations remain available without obligatory citations or instruction. Added a bounded encrypted reference library with freshness, revision IDs, correction quarantine and explicit operator reopening.

Public discovery now follows optional curiosity derived from continuity instead of random preset topics. Interleaving prevents timeline volume from hiding search observations. Selection receives parent-thread context, and original posting requires a concrete proposed contribution. Aggregate diagnostics distinguish missing/filtered opportunities, chosen silence, invalid evidence and rejected review without storing private explanations.

Removed several barriers to relevant discussion: substring keyword collisions, blanket author-name exclusions, short-post rejection, bans on relevant source links and contextual discussion of AI identity. Consequential personalized advice, fabricated experience and unsupported factual assertions are rejected by the intended semantic review policy.

## Validation

183 isolated tests passed locally on Windows/Python 3.13, including 23 new discussion tests. Existing notifier integration tests now mock the reviewed generator boundary. The billing integration test expects one charge for drafting and one for review, with no duplicate runner charge. The former blanket AI-disclosure rejection test now permits contextual AI discussion, while assistant-jargon checks remain.

Offline status and dry-run commands returned success. SHA-256 snapshots confirmed all 20 production files were unchanged. Both catalogue pages were retrieved in read-only checks. No live tick, paid generation, social publication, or operator test message was triggered.

## Limits

Exact source IDs and quotes establish provenance, not guaranteed truth. Semantic review is probabilistic and may use the same model as drafting. Research is restricted to reviewed HTTPS source hosts, with two initial catalogue pages; it is not general web search. Other users' images remain uninspected. Human comparison of Korean and English conversations and ongoing production observation remain necessary. No dialogue scripts, posting/reply ratios, or new spending caps were introduced.
