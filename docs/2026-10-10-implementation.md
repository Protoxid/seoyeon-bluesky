# Evidence-led continuity implementation

This change follows the [project review](2026-10-10-project-review-and-roadmap.md). That review is a historical baseline, not the current feature specification.

Implemented: isolated offline CLI/test execution; context-driven model action choice over eligible candidates; no automatic life-progress or count-based intimacy; optional creative artifacts/drafts with revisions; evidence-scoped encrypted memories and commitments; provenance validation and correction history; no canned generator failure output; explicit POV model routing; delivered-pixel review; atomic and serialized budget reservations with per-attempt billing; durable social outbox; authenticated encrypted operator inbox; private log redaction; encrypted legacy migration; documented recovery and public-work export.

The old fixed routine, seeded October projects, compulsory night selfies and topic percentages were removed. Tests that demanded those behaviors now check the replacement contract. Operational caps and privacy/delivery rules remain deterministic by design.

Important limits: semantic interpretation and vision review are model judgments; local tests mock external providers; paid preview costs are outside the production ledger; an ambiguous provider request can require operator reconciliation; historical public logs/commits may retain previously exposed excerpts. The old simulator does not measure naturalness. A continuous event-driven worker, adaptive model price catalog, automatic invoice/task reconciliation, and blinded longitudinal editorial review remain future work. No extra plugin is required for this implementation.
