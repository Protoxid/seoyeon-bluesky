## THE DISCLOSURE DECISION, AND WHY IT IS NOT A COST

Two facts are already settled and neither is optional:

1. **Fanvue requires it.** "Every AI creator has an AI tag displayed in their
   profile bio" and disclosure must be "clear and prominent". Failure "may
   result in your content being removed and/or your account being suspended."
2. **The reels carry Meta's AI label regardless.** Meta requires disclosure for
   "photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created
   or altered". Every reel we publish is inside that.

So the label is arriving on the content whether the bio mentions it or not. A
bio that hides what the posts already declare reads as a bait-and-switch to
anyone who follows the funnel from Instagram to Fanvue and finds the AI tag
waiting there.

**"Looks artificial" and "is disclosed as AI" are different problems.** The
mission has always been that the renders must not look fake — bad skin, plastic
lighting, uncanny hands. That is a craft problem and it is where the work goes.
A line in the bio does not make a good render look worse. It sets expectations
once, at the door, and then the content is judged on whether it is good.

Bluesky adds its own reason: their impersonation rule permits accounts that
"identify their nature in both display name and bio". Disclosure there is the
compliant path, not a choice.

## AGE IS 25 — CORRECTED TWICE, 5 AND 6 SEP 2026
First draft said 24. I corrected it to 26 from `identity_block.txt`. Both
were wrong for a bio. `character.md` had already resolved this deliberately:
she was born 23 Oct 2000, so **the account states 25 until her birthday and 26
after it**, while `identity_block.txt` says 26 because every image descends
from it and two months is invisible in a render.
**26 in prompts. 25 in bios, captions and chat.** See CANON.md §2.

Not a cosmetic slip. Age is the detail a stranger cross-references between
profiles, and a persona whose stated age drifts DOWNWARD across platforms is
the exact pattern Fanvue's age-baiting rule exists to catch. One number, one
value, everywhere.

## FANVUE  — public bio must be SFW; disclosure mandatory; no age-baiting
```
서울 · living alone in seongsu
an ai creator — everything here is made, not photographed
the parts i don't put on instagram
```
*122 characters.* "Made, not photographed" is the disclosure and it is also the
most flattering possible framing of it: it claims craft rather than confessing a
shortcut.

**Banned from this bio by Fanvue's AUP §2.6:** any age-baiting language — "just
turned 18", "barely legal", "teen" — and any implied nudity in the avatar,
banner or intro video. First offence removes the account from Discover.
