From generating W37 batch 1 (6 Sep 2026, 4 images, $0.36):

- **"Draw the curtains" is a lighting decision, not a detail.** My `w37_fan`
  prompt said the grey curtains were "drawn against the afternoon"; the render
  came back looking like dusk and took the flat off its own plate, where the
  curtains sit bunched at either side. Re-rolled with "curtains pushed back to
  either side, afternoon lying flat and bright on the glass and in a pale band
  across the floorboards" — hot, 15:10, recognisable. Carry heat with light,
  never by closing the room down.
- **Motion sells a running fan.** "Blades spun into a soft blur" rendered the
  blur. A static-looking fan is the difference between "the fan has been on all
  day" and a product shot of a fan.
- **Unbranded packaging: ask for plain, not for "no logo".** The gimbap tray
  came back clear plastic, the barley-tea bottle with no label at all, and the
  shop behind out of focus — a clean pass on the legible-packaging rule with no
  negations naming the artifact. (Same lesson as SKIN: naming a thing summons it.)
- **Plate + face references work.** gpt-image-2 put her *in* the studio plate
  (windows left, mirror right, water cooler, shoe rack) with identity held
  against `master/a/a1_front.png` + `a2_tq_left.png`. Continuity frames do not
  cost the face.
- **A sports-bra floor selfie is in family with what already shipped**
  (`w2_class_two`) — no body reference, no mention of the tattoo, knees drawn
  up. It reads as a woman on a floor after class, not as achievement.
- **Batch discipline paid for itself.** Two shots were checkpointed into
  `handoff.json` before the third re-roll started, so a quota wall mid-reroll
  would have lost nothing.
