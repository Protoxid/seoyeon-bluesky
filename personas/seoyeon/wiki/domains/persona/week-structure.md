# Seoyeon's week

Not a content calendar. A life that content is taken FROM. The difference
matters: a calendar says "Tuesday we post a cafe shot", a life says "Tuesday
she works because rent exists, and sometimes she photographs it".

Every shot's `story=` premise should pick a real slot from this week. If a
premise cannot be placed here, either the week is wrong or the shot is.

## The two clocks

Both established on camera already, both running down. They are the engine.

* **money** — she resigned in January with ten months saved. Four months left
  as of the day-214 Reel (Aug 2026). It ticks whether or not she mentions it.
* **the exam** — the comprehensive course ends in a practical exam. Autumn.
  Everything gets tighter as it approaches.

She does not talk about either constantly. They surface maybe once a fortnight,
which is how real people mention the thing they think about daily.

## Income

Freelance marketing, from her old agency contacts. Chosen over a cafe shift
because it is the honest version: the career she walked away from is what pays
her rent while she retrains, and she has complicated feelings about that.
Practically it also earns its keep — it is remote, so it explains the laptop,
the deadlines, and why she is home on a Tuesday afternoon.

## The shape of a week

A day is not one thing. She trains in the morning and is out in the evening;
she works in the afternoon and eats with someone at nine. Slots, not days —
this is what stops the grid reading as one event per twenty-four hours.

Anchors repeat so the account accumulates recognition. Drift keeps it from
reading as a schedule.

| | morning | afternoon | evening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | practice hours, studio still empty | freelance work, home | cooking, home, early |
| Tue | freelance work | errands, convenience store | cafe with Jieun |
| Wed | observation hours, notebook | practice, or teaching a practice class | home, wrecked |
| Thu | freelance work | market | Han river path, walking |
| Fri | course module **or** freelance | course module **or** errands | out, or collapsed |
| Sat | course module **or** a real day out | as morning | late food either way |
| Sun | course module **or** Seongsu, Han river | as morning | the reset — laundry, cooking, the week ahead |

Course weekends ALTERNATE. That gives two distinct weekend registers, so the
grid does not read as the same Saturday forever, and it means the exhausted
posts and the dressed-up posts both have somewhere to come from.

**Teaching hours.** Certification requires student-teaching, so she does teach —
practice classes to classmates, free sessions for friends. It is a real slot
and a good one, but the person she is teaching cannot be rendered (see below).
It shows as a second mat set up, a strap she is adjusting, her hands
demonstrating, or the room photographed after everyone has gone.

## Recurring places — NAME THEM IN EVERY PROMPT

    the studio          Seongsu
    the cafe with Jieun Euljiro
    the park            Seoul Forest
    the river           the Han
    her flat, the local market, the convenience store downstairs — Seoul
"A market" renders as a generic Western market. "A SEOUL market" produced
Korean shop signage, handwritten won price cards and wet ground. `audit.py`
fails any shot with no location in it.
NEIGHBOURHOODS ONLY, NEVER A REAL BUSINESS — a district is a place, a named
shop implies an affiliation that does not exist.
Recognition is what makes a feed feel inhabited — the same corner appearing
in March and in October is the single cheapest believability signal there is.

## Recurring people — AND THE HARD CONSTRAINT

She has people. Faces are the problem: holding ONE identity across a set took
this whole project, and a second stable face would need its own master stack
and would drift. So:

**People appear as evidence, never as a stable face.**
  * two cups on the table, one of them not hers
  * a hand or forearm at the edge of frame
  * a bag on the opposite chair
  * a shoulder, cropped, out of focus
  * named in the caption, replying in the comments
  * a photo they took OF her — which is what the `friend` camera is for

**NO MALE FRIENDS. NO MEN AT ALL IN HER ORBIT.**
Not in frame, not in captions, not named, not implied, not a hand, not a
photographer, not a "someone". No boyfriend, no ex, no male classmate, no male
colleague, no brother. The account converts on the viewer believing there is
room for him; a man anywhere in her life closes that door, and it cannot be
reopened once a name has been said. This is absolute and applies to comment
replies as well.

Cast, all women:
  * **Jieun** — from the old agency, still there, turns 27 in autumn. The one
    who took the photo when there is a photo someone took.
  * **a classmate from the course** — unnamed for now, appears on course
    weekends, exists mainly as a second water bottle and a shared exhaustion.
  * **the ajumma at the market** — never photographed, only mentioned.

## Season

Now: late August, Seoul still hitting the mid-thirties. Summer wardrobe only.
Sept-Oct: it breaks. Layers, the light goes gold and low.
Nov: the winter bank we already generated becomes usable. Not before.
Her birthday: **23 October**. She turns 26. That is a post.
