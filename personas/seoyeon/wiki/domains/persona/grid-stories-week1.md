# The week behind the grid — Mon 17 to Sun 23 August 2026

She is 25 (26 on 23 October). She resigned in January with ten months saved.
Four months left. The certification exam is in autumn. Course weekends
alternate; this is an off weekend. Seoul is in the last week of the heat.

Twelve photographs, one week, in the order they were TAKEN. Threads run
between them. Nobody will consciously notice the threads. That is the point.

---

## MON 17 AUG · 07:04 · the studio, before anyone
**`n_studio_dawn`**
**No face in it.**

Trainees get the seven o'clock slot because nobody else wants it. She has a
key. She is the only person in the building and she has not washed her face.
She unrolls her mat and then sits on the floor for a minute doing nothing,
because at this hour the sun comes through the east window and lies in a hard
band across the boards, and that is the one part of the arrangement she would
keep. She photographs the floor. Not herself — there is nothing to say about
how she looks at 7am, and everything to say about the room.

*Expression: none. She is not in it.*
*Thread: the mat and the bag are hers, and the towel is the one that turns up
on Wednesday.*

## MON 17 AUG · 16:20 · the table, work finished
**`n_desk_late`**
**No face. A knee and a bare foot at the bottom edge.**

Freelance afternoon — a deck due Wednesday for the agency she used to work at
full-time. She sat down at one and it is now twenty past four. The coffee she
made at two is cold with a ring dried inside the mug. She shuts the laptop and
the low sun is coming in flat across the table, and the table looks better than
anything she did on it. So she photographs the table. Her own knee is in the
shot because she did not move.

*Expression: none.*
*Thread: the fan on the floor is pointed at her. It is 34 degrees.*

## TUE 18 AUG · 21:15 · a cafe in Euljiro, with Jieun
**`n_cafe_jieun`**
**Jieun takes it. Mid-sentence, mouth open on a word, one hand still raised.**

Jieun is avoiding going home and they have been at the same table since eight.
Seoyeon is talking herself into Wednesday — she is teaching a practice class
for the first time and she is more nervous than she is admitting. She says
something she thinks is funny and Jieun picks up her phone without warning.
She posts it because she likes how she looks when she is not the one holding
the camera, and because it is evidence she still sees people.

*Expression: caught talking. Mouth open on a consonant, eyebrows up, one hand
mid-gesture. Not a pose — an interruption.*
*Thread: two glasses, the second one nearest the lens, because that is where
Jieun is sitting.*

## WED 19 AUG · 14:30 · the studio, straight after
**`n_studio_taught`**
**Selfie. Wrecked and pleased and it does not read as a smile.**

She taught the practice class. It went fine. She got one cue wrong and
corrected it out loud and nobody minded. The room is still busy — two other
women on mats, not looking at her. She is sitting on her mat with her forearm
thrown out toward the phone, sweat at her hairline, and she takes one because
she wants it recorded that she did it. What comes back is not a triumphant
face. It is a completely blank one, which is what "I did the thing and now I
am empty" actually looks like.

*Expression: blank. Chin down, mouth closed, eyes level, flushed. Sweat on the
forehead and along the hairline.*
*Thread: the towel from Monday morning is on the mat behind her.*

## WED 19 AUG · 23:10 · the convenience store downstairs
**`n_conv_yawn`**

Eleven hours after the class she goes down for something to eat. The shop is
lit like an operating theatre. She catches herself in the freezer glass and
thinks, against expectation, that she looks fine — so she takes one, deadpan,
and goes back upstairs.

*Expression: mouth closed and pushed slightly to one side, one eyebrow
marginally higher than the other, eyes level. Unimpressed with the day, not
with herself.*
*Thread: it is eleven hours since the class. She is done.*

## THU 20 AUG · 15:40 · the market
**`n_market`**
**No face. Her shoulder blocks one corner.**

Thirty-four degrees. She walked because the delivery minimum costs more than
she wants to spend. The peaches this week are absurd — 20,000 won for six,
handwritten on a card — and she stands in front of the stall long enough that
the woman running it looks at her. She photographs the fruit. This is the
picture on the grid that is not about her at all.

*Expression: none.*
*Thread: she buys the peaches. They are in the bag twenty-five minutes later.*

## THU 20 AUG · 16:05 · her own street, carrying it home
**`n_street_bag`**
**Selfie. Mouth pressed flat, eyebrows up. Not enjoying this.**

The bag is cutting into her fingers, her shirt is stuck to her back, and she is
halfway up a hill she chose to walk. She stops in the shade of a building with
the sunlit road burning out behind her and takes one, because it is ridiculous,
because she still looks fine, and because the heat is the only thing anyone in
Seoul is talking about this week.

*Expression: exasperated. Mouth pressed into a flat line, eyebrows up, damp
hair stuck at her temples. The face of someone regretting a decision.*
*Thread: the bag in her hand holds the peaches from 15:40.*

## THU 20 AUG · 19:30 · the Han river path
**`n_river_sun`**

She walks the river most Thursdays after errands. The sun goes down behind the
bridge and the whole surface turns, and it is so obvious, so postcard, so
exactly the thing everyone photographs — that she photographs it anyway.

**She is not in this one.** The honest version of "the light was unbelievable"
is a picture of the light. Putting herself in front of it would have made it a
picture about her instead, and there were already five of those.

*Expression: none. No face.*
*Thread: the last thing before the course weekend swallows Fri to Sun.*

## FRI 21 AUG · 20:50 · a place near the studio
**`n_noodles`**
**No face. One hand and a pair of chopsticks.**

Course weekend starts tomorrow. She eats at the place around the corner from
the studio because it is the last proper meal before three long days. The food
comes and it is very good and very hot and she photographs it from directly
above with her hand already in the frame, because she is not going to let it go
cold for a photograph.

*Expression: none.*
*Thread: the last quiet evening in the week.*
*Rebuilt 25 Aug. The first render put her in frame from shoulder to thigh and,
because a limb shot carries the body reference, dressed her in the reference's
bone-white sportswear -- in a public noodle shop. The fix was not better
clothing. It was keeping her out of the picture: a forearm, and the rest of her
past the edge.*

## SAT 22 AUG · 18:40 · the hallway, before Jieun's birthday
**`n_mirror_dress`**
**Mirror selfie. The phone is visible. Blank, appraising.**

Off weekend, so no course module. Jieun turns 27 and there is dinner. Seoyeon
almost never dresses up and tonight she does, and she wants it recorded before
the evening ruins it. She stands in the hallway mirror with the whole flat
behind her — shoes left out, a coat on a hook, a laundry rack through the
doorway — and takes one. She is not smiling at herself. She is checking.

*Expression: flat and appraising. The face people make at their own reflection
when they are deciding whether it is working. Slightly narrowed eyes, mouth
closed and neutral.*
*Thread: the same dress appears five hours later.*

## SAT 22 AUG · 23:50 · the roof of her building, home from dinner
**`n_roof_posed`**
**Propped. She wanted the whole outfit in it, so she put the phone down.**

She is back before midnight because she has practice hours in the morning. She
is not ready to go inside. She goes up to the roof in the dress, sets the phone
on a ledge, walks into frame and poses properly — the only reason worth the
effort of putting a phone down and walking away from it is wanting your whole
outfit in the picture. The framing is wrong because she could not check it.

*Expression: none she chose. She is waiting for the shutter, mouth closed.
Standing still for a timer is not the same thing as posing.*
*Thread: dress and bag from 18:40. Water tanks and ducting behind her, and the
washing still on the drying rack.*
*Rebuilt 25 Aug. The first render was a fashion editorial: magenta sky, softbox
light on her at midnight, dead centre, legs long, no grain at all. Three of my
own phrases caused it -- "magenta dusk cast" painted a studio backdrop, "posed
deliberately / chin lifted / small closed smile" is a direction you give a
model, and "everything sharp" is a daylight rule that manufactured impossible
clarity at midnight.*

## SUN 23 AUG · 17:20 · Seoul Forest, on the grass with Jieun
**`n_grass_laugh`**
**Selfie. Mid-laugh, teeth showing, eyes creased shut.**

They sat on the grass for an hour doing very little. Jieun says something about
the exam and Seoyeon laughs properly — the ugly kind, head back, eyes gone. She
takes the photo in the middle of it and keeps the one where she looks terrible,
because it is the only frame this week where she looks happy.

*Expression: a real laugh. Teeth showing, eyes creased shut, head tipped back,
chin doubled slightly. The opposite of every other frame.*
*Thread: closes the week. Jieun's third appearance.*
