## Bio

150 characters. The manual "AI-generated" line is GONE — Instagram's own
AI Creator label appears in the bio area and under the username on every post
and Reel, including in Explore, reading "This profile posts content that was
generated or modified with AI." Writing it again wastes the scarcest space on
the profile.

**Enable the AI Creator toggle, and treat it as mandatory.** Meta auto-applies
the label when it detects AI signals, so declining does not keep it off — it
only makes the badge look like Meta caught you rather than you declaring it,
which is worse for exactly the trust this account runs on. The operator is
EU-based and the AI Act transparency deadline has passed, so this is now a
requirement rather than a choice. There is no algorithmic penalty for carrying
the label; reach only moves on behaviour.

```
25. seoul.
retraining as a pilates instructor, slowly.
mostly alone, mostly fine.
dm me, i actually reply.
```

Why each line:

**"25. seoul."** — age and city, the two facts that decide whether a stranger
keeps reading. Lowercase, full stops, no flourish. **Change to 26 on 23
October** — a stale bio is a small tell, and the change is a reason to post.

**"retraining as a pilates instructor, slowly."** — explains the body and the
free days without making the account about fitness. "Slowly" is the character
in one word: dry, unhurried, faintly self-deprecating.

**"mostly alone, mostly fine."** — the line that creates the opening. It says
she is available in the parasocial sense without saying anything explicit, and
the repeated "mostly" is exactly her register.

**"dm me, i actually reply."** — bought with the characters the disclosure was
using, and worth more than any of them. The DM ladder is where the revenue is,
and this line recruits directly into step one of it. "Actually" does the work:
every other account in this space has an unread inbox, and she is claiming the
opposite. It has to be true — protect the DM hour.

Fanvue has its own AI disclosure requirement; declare it there per their rules.
The Instagram label does not travel to other platforms, screenshots or reposts.

## The DM ladder

Where the revenue is. Protect the hour.

1. **Story reply arrives** — she answers within a few hours, once, short,
   in voice. No pitch.
2. **Second exchange** — she asks something back. One question, specific.
3. **Warm** — mentions the free page in passing, as a fact rather than an offer.
4. **Ask** — only after they have replied three times unprompted.
5. **Paid** — the ask is one line, never repeated in the same conversation.

The failure mode is pitching at step 1. Ninety percent of accounts do it and it
is why their DMs convert at nothing.
