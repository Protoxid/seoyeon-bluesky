**post 1 ends up bottom-right and post 12 ends up top-left.**

Spread them over two or three sittings across an evening and a morning. Twelve
posts landing in ten minutes on a new account is the commonest bot pattern
there is, and spacing them costs nothing.

Alt text sits under **Advanced settings → Write alt text**. It matters more
than hashtags now: Mosseri has said hashtags never meaningfully drove reach,
while search runs on captions and image content. Write it as a plain
description with the place in it — never a keyword list.

Location tags: real, specific, matching the photograph. **Use the Korean
name** — that is what the place is called and what Instagram lists it under,
and Korean creators writing in English still tag Korean locations in Korean.
Search for the Hangul in the location picker; the English romanisation often
returns nothing or a different pin.

## Hashtag policy

Two per post at most, and only where there is a real place to name. Nothing on
the home, body or quiet frames — a hashtag on an intimate post reads as reach-
chasing and undercuts it.

Mosseri: *"Hashtags work, but they've never been a good way to actually
increase your reach"* — helping "ever so slightly on the margins" at best. They
are labels, not distribution. The work is done by the place names sitting in
plain words inside the captions and the alt text: seongsu, the han, seoul
forest, kalguksu.

Korean-script tags (`#서울`, `#성수동`) are worth including on place posts. They
cost nothing, they read as native, and they reach the local search surface that
the English ones do not.

## Two global fixes applied after the first batch

**Face highlight.** Every generated frame had a wet specular sheen on the
cheekbones, nose and brow — the model's default "glass skin". It reads as heavy
highlighter, it was uniform across shots so it compounded, and it is one of the
more reliable AI tells. `outside.SKIN` now appends to every prompt: matte, the
light sinking in rather than sitting on top, a faint sheen only along the nose
and the inner eye corner. Completely matte is its own tell, so a little is kept.
`h_sofa_evening` had also literally asked for "slightly shiny" skin — removed,
and `audit.py` now flags shine words.

**Extra limbs on close selfies.** `outside.ARMS` appends to every selfie:
"exactly two arms in the picture, the one extended toward the camera and one
other." Counting them is the only instruction that has held.

Anything generated before this needs regenerating to pick both up.
