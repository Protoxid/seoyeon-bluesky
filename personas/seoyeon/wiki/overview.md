# Overview

Three domains, and the boundary between them is what changes when.

## persona — slowest moving
Who she is, what she owns, where she goes, what happened to her this week.
Changes when her life changes, which is rarely and deliberately. A contradiction
here is the most expensive kind: no single frame looks wrong, and the account
stops reading as a person. That is how a rich-flat plate set got caught — good
images, wrong home, and the captions said otherwise in public.

## pipeline — fastest moving
Models, prompts, field names, gates, prices. Changes whenever a provider does.
`playbook.md` is 149 numbered rules and every one cost a failed generation.
Read the rule before overriding it.

## publishing — weekly
What goes out, when, with what words, and why a reel exists at all. The rules
here are about a reader who has never heard of her: completion and sends, not
likes.

The first measurement arrived 31 Aug and it was about completion, exactly as
expected: R1 took 118 views, **6 seconds of a 23.5-second reel**, and zero of
everything else. The first six seconds were a title card. Nothing was learned
about the content because almost nobody reached it — which is the second rule
of this domain: **a metric can only judge what was actually seen.**

## The invariant across all three
**Photorealism, and the impression of a real person.** Every rule in the
playbook is downstream of one of those two.
